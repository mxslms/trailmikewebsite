**Dates:** August 5 – 10, 2018  
**Route:** Lake McDonald → Gunsight Lake → Gunsight Pass → Lake Ellen Wilson → Sperry → raft  
**Crew:** family trail crew plus Grandpa — girls already declaring they love this

Gunsight Pass trail, east to west. First night at Lake McDonald, shuttle to the trailhead, then three nights in the backcountry and a whitewater finish. Planning docs may show up later; this is what we did.

## Day 1 — Arrive, Lake McDonald

Good flights, no drama. Picked up GPS at 7:18, parked the red lot by 8. Cabin in the park by 3:30 — relaxing, fun first day.

![Family at the Glacier National Park entrance sign](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-05-01.webp)

West Glacier restaurant for dinner. Montana BLT with Canadian bacon — good. (No tax on food or drinks. No tolls in the whole state. Noted.)

Swam in Lake McDonald. Cold. Worth it. Grabbed the permit, bear spray, and stove fuel. Easy start before the packs get real.

## Day 2 — Shuttle to Gunsight Lake (~6.2 mi)

Shuttle to the trailhead was packed. On trail around 1:00, camp around 5:15 — about 6.2 miles, 500 up / 500 down. Gorgeous view toward Gunsight Pass, waterfalls, fish jumping. Camp to ourselves. Met Cheryl and Keira. Two moose for the wildlife board.

![Grandpa and the girls on trail with Gunsight Pass ahead](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-06-01.webp)

![Evening on the lake shore — puffy jackets, waterfalls across the water](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-06-02.webp)

Great hike. Everyone tired, everyone having fun.

## Day 3 — Over Gunsight Pass to Lake Ellen Wilson

Most beautiful day of the trip. Perfect weather. Flowers everywhere. Waterfalls. Got reception at the pass and called Oscar (tried Kate — she was working).

![Crossing the suspension bridge — mountain goat on the bank below](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-01.webp)

![High trail above turquoise water and folded rock walls](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-02.webp)

![Mountain goat on a ridge with snow patches and melt streams](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-03.webp)

![Crossing a sun-cupped snowfield on the pass approach](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-04.webp)

![The girls above the lake — packs on, grins on](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-05.webp)

![Looking down on the long alpine lake from the high trail](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-06.webp)

Campsite came with our own private beach. Swam. Soooo cold. Girls say they love doing this. Grandpa having fun too — less strenuous than last year.

![Tents at the lake — private beach energy](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-07-07.webp)

## Day 4 — Lake Ellen Wilson to Sperry

Hot day, shorter hike, still a climb. Burned forest on the way — stark and strangely beautiful after the green days.

![Trail through the burn zone toward Sperry](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-08-01.webp)

Sperry Chalet for the win: turkey and Swiss grilled, **lemonade**, apple crisp. Cards at camp. Mountain goats for the evening show.

![Grandpa and the girls dealing cards at camp](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-08-02.webp)

![Grandpa selfie with a whole goat committee on the rocks](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-08-03.webp)

## Day 5 — Hike out

Last miles through the burn and rock, packs pointed west. The girls still smiling for the camera — which is how you know a trip worked.

![Hiking out through blackened snags and open valley haze](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-09-01.webp)

## Day 6 — Whitewater

Whitewater rafting to close it out. Yellow raft, turquoise water, everyone paddling. Very fun.

![Full crew in a yellow raft hitting the rapids](/images/glacier-2018/glacier-2018-08-10-01.webp)

If you’re looking for a Glacier classic that still feels like a family trip: Gunsight east-to-west delivers lakes, a pass day you’ll talk about for years, Sperry lemonade when you need it, and enough wildlife to fill the scrapbook.
