**Dates:** July 28 – August 3, 2024  
**Route:** Ptarmigan → Elizabeth → Cosley → Red Gap → Poia (Cracker Lake skipped)  
**Crew:** a tired, happy family that somehow still wanted more trail at the end

## Day 1 — Arrive, Logan Pass tease, Red Eagle

Up at 3:35 AM. Good flights — Jordan’s first plane. Rented a minivan and drove ~5 hours on wide-open Montana roads with almost no traffic. Hit the permit office before it closed.

A ranger pointed us at a short Logan Pass / Highline stretch — about a half mile for the view — so a wiped crew drove into the park and did exactly that. Amazing just driving in. The short hike was awesome; goats and marmots for the welcome committee.

Cooler than we expected. Pants for hiking. Maybe a hat. Mexican food was meh. Red Eagle Motel, Grizzly cabin — not much room, so Shilling slept outside.

## Day 2 — Ptarmigan Tunnel to Elizabeth Lake (~10.5 mi)

Up 5:30, packing mode. On trail around 9:15. Tough uphill right away — then scenery you can’t really describe. Huge climb to the tunnel. Everyone crushed it. The downhill trashed the legs.

![Line of backpackers climbing a steep trail under big Glacier peaks](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-29-01.webp)

![High cliffside trail cut into red-and-gray rock with lingering snow below](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-29-02.webp)

Elizabeth Lake Foot camp was amazing. Napped on the rock beach. Pesto for dinner — crew approved. Lights out around 8:30.

![Feet on driftwood looking across a windy alpine lake to steep peaks](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-29-03.webp)

Then the night: wind screaming off the lake into camp, plus rain. Tarp survived. Watch said sleep roughly 10:30–6. Not bad.

## Day 3 — Cosley Lake (short day)

Light mileage. Everyone still sore. Took it easy in the morning, hit Mist Falls on the way (went to the bottom — worth it), then Cosley.

Lake was beautiful. Swam. So cold. So refreshing.

![Swimming in clear turquoise Cosley Lake under a steep mountain wall](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-30-01.webp)

Beans and rice for dinner — Winnie called it favorite. Kids doing so great, getting along, having fun. They already want to hike every year.

Pit toilets: stinky. Wind again, but camp tucked in the woods on the side of the lake — tents mostly fine, and the sound was cool.

## Day 4 — Cosley to Poia via Red Gap Pass (~13.9 mi)

Big-time ab ripper. I hate it… but I love it.

Holy crap that was hard. They all did it. Nora in beast mode. Shilling said hardest thing he’s ever done. Keegan kept pushing to the top. Winnie and I buddied up. We also partnered stretches with two hikers from Michigan who were nervous about bears — they’d already seen two close to the trail.

![Resting on red rock at Red Gap Pass with a forested valley below](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-31-02.webp)

![Four backpackers crossing a high ridge with snowy peaks beyond](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-31-03.webp)

Poia Lake was gorgeous. Jordan and I swam. Sooo cold. Evening light on the water was ridiculous.

![Sunset selfie on a pebbly shore with pink clouds over the lake](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-31-01.webp)

![Sunburst over the lake from camp — golden path on the water](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-07-31-04.webp)

Also: “Eat more kale dude” energy on trail. Watch out.

## Day 5 — Hike out, skip Cracker, Highline victory lap

Hike out was tough too — steep downhill for miles. Winnie was sick; Nora was cramping hard. Group call: skip the Cracker Lake night.

I hitchhiked toward the lodge with one of the Michigan hikers. A Brit in a white Mustang picked us up — and a black bear crossed the road right in front of us.

Then the reward: Highline Trail, ~8 miles out and back, **without packs**. Amazing. Much nicer when you’re not hauling the kitchen. Drove the rest of Going-to-the-Sun. Cabin on Lake McDonald, skipped rocks, tight squeeze, laughed about it. Sunflower Cafe for dinner — soooo good.

![Celebrating on the Highline — poles up, valley and peaks wide open](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-08-01-01.webp)

![Ordering at The Sunflower Cafe under the painted mountain-and-sunflower sign](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-08-01-02.webp)

## Day 6 — Rafting, bridge jump, drive to Helena

Sunflower again for breakfast (beagle Chief sighting). Whitewater rafting — very fun. Jumped off the old entrance bridge. Wow.

![Crew on the old entrance bridge over clear turquoise water](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-08-02-02.webp)

Drove back toward Helena through gorgeous forest and the Swan Lake area. Packed the minivan like a puzzle cube. Hotel pool. Burger King. No notes.

![Minivan packed wall-to-wall with packs, shoes, and trail debris](/images/glacier-2024/glacier-2024-08-02-01.webp)
