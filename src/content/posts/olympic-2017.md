**Dates:** August 7 – 12, 2017  
**Route:** Lake Quinault → Shi Shi / Seafield Creek → Sol Duc → mountain lake → ferry to Seattle  
**Shape of the week:** a few days of Pacific beach, a stretch of rainforest and alpine lake, then a ferry into the city

## Day 1 — Travel to Lake Quinault (Aug 7)

Up at 3:30. Red lot 6B. Long travel day — connecting through Nashville — but good company makes the miles easy.

![Southwest wingtip over a snow peak punching through the cloud deck](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-07-01.webp)

Hotel around 4pm at Rain Forest Village Resort — great view, little merchant shops nearby. Salmon House for dinner (ordered the salmon, obviously). Local brews including Alaskan Amber. Sunset over Lake Quinault. Great evening.

## Day 2 — Shi Shi beach (tough, beautiful)

Up at 3am local like a light switch. Logging-road approach thanks to Google Maps. Then the beach: ropes, rocks, tide timing, hunting for the campsite.

Worth every scramble. Starfish. Anemones. Fog and beauty.

![Descending a steep forest trail toward a sandy clearing](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-08-01.webp)

![Climbing the rocky shoreline under a cliff cave in the fog](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-08-02.webp)

![Tide pool — green anemones, mussels, and rockweed](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-08-03.webp)

Tiring day. So fun.

## Day 3 — All day at Seafield Creek

Awesome day. Campfire on the driftwood. Tide pools. A dead shark for the scrapbook. Otters in Ozette Creek. Another rocky beach hike — no ropes this time, shorter miles, still a workout.

![Bright red starfish in a clear tide pool](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-09-01.webp)

![Campfire, ukulele, and fog on the beach](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-09-03.webp)

![Two packs walking the foggy shore past a sea stack](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-09-05.webp)

## Day 4 — Hike out, Hungry Bear, Sol Duc

Grindy hike out. Then civilization calories: **Hungry Bear Cafe** — one-pound burgers and a mountain of fries.

![The Hungry Bear burger with the knife through it and a pile of fries](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-10-01.webp)

Sol Duc Hot Springs to replenish — pool, hot-spring water, showers. Reset button hit.

## Day 5 — Rainforest to mountain lake

Breakfast at In-N-Out, then another long logging-road drive and another tough hike. Not stressful like the first beach day — just long. Beautiful forest the whole way.

Trail folks thought I was crazy for taking Dad on this as his first backpacking trip. (He did great.)

![Family pause at a log bridge over a clear creek](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-11-02.webp)

Campsite by a lake in the mountains. Swam. Amazing. Good dinner, early bed.

![Mountain lake under the peaks — swim-worthy green water](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-11-03.webp)

## Day 6 — Out early, ferry to Seattle

Plan was a rest day at the lake. Thunderstorms possible, and soooo many bugs. We packed up and headed out.

![Moss carpet and tall trunks on the hike out](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-12-04.webp)

Ferry into Seattle — skyline, Great Wheel, smiles on deck. Great end to the trip.

![On the ferry rail with downtown Seattle behind us](/images/olympic-2017/olympic-2017-08-12-05.webp)

Olympic in one week: Pacific fog and tide pools, rainforest hush, a cold mountain swim, and a city skyline for dessert. Hard to ask for more.
