**Dates:** August 2016  
**Stats:** ~54 miles · ~7,000 ft gain · 6 trail days  
**Arc:** West Yellowstone → Heart Lake / Mt. Sheridan → Teton Village → Death Canyon Shelf → Cascade Canyon → Jackson Lake Lodge → Yellowstone day tour → SLC

## Day 1 — Travel to West Yellowstone

We made it. Very long day — especially after riding Pelotonia the day before. Up at 3am ET, Kate to the airport, BWI connection, into SLC, then a five-hour drive. Hotel around 7:30 MT.

Hibernation Station — cool cabin, friendly check-in. Wild West Pizza for dinner; bacon pizza at Winnie’s suggestion rocked. Kids out cold. Lights out near 11:30.

## Day 2 — Yellowstone in, then into the woods

Breakfast at Running Bear Pancake House — awesome. West entrance into the park: geysers, mountains, streams. Old Faithful. Then packs on.

![Old Faithful selfie — crew and the plume](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-08-02.webp)

![Along the river with big ridges behind us](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-08-01.webp)

The hike was tougher than expected, and a lot of burn forest, so the visuals were more “character building” than postcard. Kids did great. Left my poles at a rest stop and had to run back for them. Classic.

![Heart Lake camp — evening light through the pines](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-08-03.webp)

## Day 3 — Heart Lake west + Mt. Sheridan summit

Slept well. Morning beach walk. Moved camp to the west side of Heart Lake, rested, then… **summited Mt. Sheridan**.

Amazing on top. Miles of view. Sitting at the lookout tower, Oscar’s phone buzzed — service! Called Mama. Walked around grinning.

![Summit crew with Heart Lake curved below](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-09-02.webp)

![Beaver making a wake along the shore](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-09-01.webp)

Great day.

## Day 4 — Wind night, then Tetons

Very windy overnight. Near midnight: “Dad come here.” Fifteen minutes later — scream. Winnie and Nora yelling “bear!” Bear spray out, run over… no bear. Tent cover making noises in the wind. They’d been legit terrified. We walked to the lake and looked at the stars until everyone settled. Milky Way was ridiculous. Oscar woke up to an empty camp and wondered where the heck everyone went.

![Quiet morning on the Heart Lake shore](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-10-01.webp)

Drove down to the Tetons — views awesome. Teton Village, Alpenhof Lodge. German dinner: soup, sausages, huckleberry cheesecake, brownie sundae. Still waking at 4am on the dot (6am ET), but recharged and ready for granite.

## Day 5 — Tram up, flowers all day, Death Canyon Shelf

Breakfast at Alpenhof rocked (real bacon). Another shower. Rode the Teton tram — excellent call. Beautiful trail day: elk, beaver, chipmunk-scale lunch thieves. Lunch at Marion Lake. Fox Creek Pass. Flowers everywhere.

![Looking out the tram toward the mountain and village below](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-11-01.webp)

![Yellow wildflowers and a big Teton basin from the trail](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-11-02.webp)

Sunset on the cliff by camp on Death Canyon Shelf with Winnie — then heavy stomping. Big elk running the ridge above us. Cool. Slightly spicy. Sky put on a show.

![Tent on the shelf — sunrise/sunset glow over Death Canyon](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-12-01.webp)

## Day 6 — Alaska Basin, Hurricane Pass, Cascade Canyon

Woke to sunrise over Death Canyon. Cold. Elk huffing right outside the tent. Through Alaska Basin (neat rocks), up Hurricane Pass pretty quickly, then the drop into Cascade Canyon — Schoolroom Glacier, blue-green lake, streams, waterfalls, ponds. Pictures don’t do it. Kids loved it. Best campsite of the trip. Early bed.

![Schoolroom Glacier and the turquoise lake from the high trail](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-12-02.webp)

## Day 7 — Cascade Canyon out + Jackson Lake Lodge

All downhill through Cascade. Two moose. Streams, waterfalls, huckleberries, thimbleberries, raspberries. On the drive, bison in a field.

Jackson Lake Lodge — showers, pool, drinks, outdoor BBQ (all-you-can-eat, live music). Back to the lodge to watch the Olympics. Soft sheets hit different after a week of nylon.

## Day 8 — Yellowstone greatest hits, then SLC

Kids up at 5am. Back into Yellowstone for day hikes: Artist Point, Uncle Tom’s, North Rim, Mammoth. Easy car touring, big sights. Road stopped by a bison parade — guy on a bike rode straight through and cleared a path.

![Bison owning the lane](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-14-01.webp)

![Artist Point — Lower Falls and the yellow canyon](/images/tetons-2016/tetons-2016-08-14-02.webp)

Six hours to Salt Lake City. Pizza in the hotel. Sleep.

Great trip.
