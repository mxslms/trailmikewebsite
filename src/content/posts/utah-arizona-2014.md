**Dates:** August 2014  
**Arc:** Dayton → Zion (Narrows top-down) → canyoneering → Bryce → Grand Canyon → Vegas pool → home

## Day 1 — Travel to Zion

Up at 3:30am. Flight out of Dayton. Kids sleepy, behaving nicely. Made it to Zion around 4pm local. Hamburgers and salad at camp. Early lights-out — 5am wake-up coming.

## Days 2–3 — The Narrows top-down (camp 12)

Shuttle to the top. Driver kept shouting **“That’s not Orderville!!!!!”** — kept us awake.

Chamberlain’s Ranch trailhead, then into the river. Bulls up top got our blood going. Amazing experience. Girls took a dip in the evening at camp. Barely saw anyone until near the end on day two.

## Day 5 — Bryce Jr. Rangers, then foggy Grand Canyon

Stopped at the Bryce ranger station — girls became Junior Rangers. Geology talk with a pizza metaphor for the Grand Staircase: chocolate dough, vermillion sauce, gray sardines, white mozzarella, pink sauce. Iron makes the rocks red.

![Overlook crew — hoodoos forever](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-12-06.webp)

![Among the orange spires](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-12-07.webp)

Little flash flood in the early hours. Rain so loud Oscar and I couldn’t hear each other speak.

Drove to Grand Canyon. Early evening — completely socked in fog. Barely saw any of it. Pizza at the lodge. Girls got pets: Nora (prairie dog) and Winnie (squirrel). Oscar got a Narrows poster. Good talk on the drive.

![Grand Canyon arrival — fog owning the view](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-13-01.webp)

![Evening light starting to clear the rim](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-13-02.webp)

## Day 6 — Grand Canyon, clear and grand

Morning — and we could see it. Amazing. Big. Grand.

Day hikes, camp downtime, rummy with the girls. Easy day. Monkey bread for breakfast, eggs in the dutch oven for dinner.

![Rim crew with the canyon wide open](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-14-01.webp)

![Layers on layers — the view finally showing off](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-14-02.webp)

![Rummy in the tent](/images/southwest-2014/southwest-2014-08-14-03.webp)

## Day 7 — One more rim look, then Vegas

Short morning hike behind camp for another canyon view. Drive to Vegas — pool day — then home.

Zion water, Bryce pizza geology, Grand Canyon fog-to-clear. Hard to beat that sequence.
